# Reference-led visual revision 3

The operator explicitly chose the supplied glossy toy-city and black/gold open-suitcase images as the targets. They supersede the earlier muted V2 direction. The supplied images are retained in References for comparison, not used as flattened gameplay screens.

Cashflow uses a newly generated 24-building transparent atlas, planted lots and matching portrait/wide city environments. Native roads/traffic, compatible moving resource links, a real forecast bar, always-visible basic building choices, bold Lilita One lettering and a large gold activation control sit around the authoritative puzzle board. Budget prices, eight-beat outcomes and campaign goals remain the actual domain values.

Black Bag uses its painted collector study and complete treasure art with a newly generated leather/brass rim and separate velvet lid, black/gold panels, Cinzel Decorative display type, Crimson Text body type, parchment instructions and crimson action controls. Treasures preserve their full illustration on exact shaped mounts. Their legal occupied cells, rotation, alert and value are unchanged. The new six-tool prop sheet is retained as authored art; the current tool roll remains native text controls.

Responsive native uGUI layouts fill the laptop window and phone safe area. Wide play uses a centered board with side controls; portrait play uses a full-height composition. F11 switches laptop fullscreen/windowed. Short Black Bag layouts scale the composition together, retaining square cells and proportional illustrations. Text stays native and independently rendered. Original synthesized V3 music is more rhythmic (Cashflow 106 BPM, Black Bag 96 BPM), with existing tactile cues, important-cue ducking, saved mutes and reduced-motion controls.

All artwork was generated with the built-in image tool and copied as project assets. EXECUTED_PROMPTS.json records the seven reference-driven generation requests. The Black Bag panoramic study was generated earlier in the same session and retained; its source is exec-d8517026-7f94-48ea-8796-4750e5adc1a9.png in the session generated-images folder. Its portrait study and treasure atlas remain V2 originals. Runtime PNG pixels were not post-edited; atlas trimming uses metadata only. No franchise art, new currency, fake earnings rate or new game mechanic is introduced. Named inspiration games were mood examples, not copied assets.

Built-in generation mode was used without a CLI or external image service. Asset/font hashes are in ../ORIGINAL_ASSETS.json; fonts and OFL licenses are under Resources/Fonts/V3 and Docs/Licenses. Actual accepted player captures and test reports are linked from ../REVIEW.html and ../MOBILE_ACCEPTANCE.md. Engineering checks do not establish player preference, retention or revenue.
