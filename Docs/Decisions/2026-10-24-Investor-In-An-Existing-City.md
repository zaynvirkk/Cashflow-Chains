# Investor in an established city

The latest operator instruction supersedes empty-city founding and the mandatory home→café opening. The player enters an existing city to develop a private portfolio. Civic buildings,other owners’ residential blocks,industry,wholesale supply,utilities and regional transport already exist. Their assets and income never belong to the player. Existing private assets,cash,entitlements and committed trading outcomes must survive the transition.

Implement a versioned regional context entered through an idempotent domain command. Frozen pre-transition trading days retain their original rules. Subsequent days include concrete,finite regional households and a regional labor pool,with public employment reserved. Only private businesses contribute rent,sales,expenses and stored income. Regional households retain real ages,budgets,preferences and one-day demand limits. Baseline municipal services provide context;do not imply a complete police,fire,medical or legal case simulation.

The private investment area expands within the same established city. Public buildings remain fixed outside the player’s plots and are clearly labeled as belonging to the city or other owners. Roads serve those actual buildings;unowned empty land does not create income. Wholesale supply is inspectable through real published material trades. An operating-day market calendar changes documented demand/import conditions;no fabricated multiplayer,live market feed or random crisis is presented.

Use a short visual career path grounded in ownership,actual positive settlement,diversification,earned cashflow and development. Preserve the latest financial previews,service/payment diagram,real construction progress and bounded camera. The café is a later opportunity rather than a required city bootstrap. The first rental asset can operate independently of owning a café. Paid commerce remains subject to existing production authority requirements;no fake purchase or entitlement is introduced by the setting change.

Acceptance must include absence of private income from public assets,finite public demand and real stock consumption,public worker reservation,independent rental operation,idempotent entry/settlement,preserved old trading promises,ownership-restricted commands,and an actual signed Android investor journey. Historical founding tests remain valid for the legacy context;new tests must cover the active investor context directly.
