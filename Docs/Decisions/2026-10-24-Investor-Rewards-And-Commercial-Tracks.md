# Investor rewards and commercial tracks — 24 October 2026

The operator explicitly requests daily free/paid rewards, milestone progression, battle passes, packs and randomized acquisition integrated with retention. This extends the existing Game Bible commercial direction. Keep the latest already-populated-city/investor experience: rewards recognize the player's private portfolio, not ownership of the public city.

This slice introduces a versioned catalog and unified claim ledger, a seven-claim daily calendar, six permanent career milestones and a 28-real-day/30-tier season. UTC dates are distinct from compressed operating days. Skipping a visit never resets calendar progress. Real archived season rewards remain claimable; a pass unlocks eligible earned tiers retroactively and never grants progress for fictional actions.

Commercial products reference these same rewards: permanent Investor Club extra daily track, permanent Growth Fund extra milestone track, one-season/nonrenewing premium track, one-time acquisition starter and repeatable development supplies. Each has actual eligibility and authored contents. No invented prices or paid success. The new products are not registered with the legacy local-profile billing adapter. Their trusted fulfillment primitive is not in the public HTTP command allowlist. Production billing requires the actual authenticated Unity account/backend integration, Play catalog/verification, durable token ownership, acknowledgments, recovery and refund reconciliation. Those remain unconfigured and checkout stays visibly unavailable. Existing legacy entitlements remain unchanged.

Earned bronze chests use the exact disclosed 10,000-basis-point table from the catalog and a cryptographic unbiased draw inside the domain transaction. The committed result is retained in reward history; the reveal never rerolls. Retry returns the original receipt; storage/save failures preserve the chest and wallet. Offline opening accepts earned containers only. Paid randomized containers, gem-funded draws, manager recruitment/pity and merchant rerolls remain separate incomplete slices; do not advertise them as working features. Plans and franchise fragments are retained for the existing development inventory; full city-franchise utility remains pending.

Policy basis checked against primary sources: [Google Play payments and randomized-item disclosure](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738?hl=en) and [purchase verification/fulfillment](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/integrate). These are requirements, not evidence of live commerce or commercial success.

No popups on entry, forced ad, streak penalty, hidden chance, near-miss animation or payment to correct a touch. One discoverable rewards hub links all tracks. Core free city play remains available. No external accounts, purchases, deployment or public release authorized by this change.
