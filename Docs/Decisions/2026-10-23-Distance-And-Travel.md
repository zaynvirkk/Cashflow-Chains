# Distance,travel and understandable city roles

The operator's23October correction explicitly supersedes mandatory one-tile directional pipe connections in the current playable city. The old rule set remains version0 for completed proofs,legacy saves and reference regressions; new active boards use version1. A rule version belongs to the board and proof fingerprint,not a presentation toggle. Existing earned rewards stay idempotent by city irrespective of version.

Version1 models a24-minute trading day. Homes generate prospective visits; farms harvest stock without a hidden startup-energy token; selling businesses consume their published customer/stock/power recipe when arrivals are available. Every shipment has a deterministic destination,departure,travel duration and arrival. Distance is Manhattan city blocks,with a one-minute minimum; depots/couriers can ship faster. Pending deliveries reserve destination demand and remain conserved at closing time. Longer journeys reduce useful selling time rather than deleting the route. Rotation is cosmetic in this mode; destination selection replaces the primary rotate control. Automatic routes prefer nearby compatible recipients with room; players can choose one recipient explicitly. Inspectors must show real journeys and arrival times,not generic 'connected' assertions.

The first three cities teach shorter customer journeys,faster stock delivery,and both together. Their targets and reference plans are versioned with the new rules. Rule0 expected outcomes and saved contracts are not rewritten to make the new system appear compatible.

Ambient traffic is visual context,not the source of simulated income. Its cars use separated opposing lanes,red-light stop lines and collision checks at intersections. Their dimensions must cover the rendered silhouettes. Native captures and deterministic traffic tests must verify movement and non-overlap.

This is a gameplay correction authorized by the operator,not an external commercial result or a claim of human comprehension. No new currency,paid reward or additional grant is introduced.
