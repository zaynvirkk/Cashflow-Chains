# Household and housing correction

The operator rejected12 residents in a single small house and asked for coherent logic. Catalog5/city-rules2 replace the previous population multiplier. The game models a household as2 adults and2 children. A single house contains one household,up to4 residents. Capacity and occupied households are distinct. Jobs and utilities support household arrivals;extra empty property does not generate residents or rent.

Housing development now provides actual additional dwellings: house1,duplex2,row homes3,small apartments4,apartment block6. The map and city miniature render two/three houses for the duplex/rows and existing original apartment art for the higher-density forms. Details show occupied homes,capacity,adults,children,rent,bills and margin. No tiny single-house sprite silently becomes a twelve-person building.

Coffee needs come from adults,at most one per adult each operating day;children generate no coffee demand. Shopping is a finite household activity;occasional service/entertainment needs rotate across adults so integer rounding does not permanently erase small-household needs. Hiring/building checks explicitly use adult availability. The starter café is a small one-employee operation with correspondingly lower capacity and operating cost;it is not balanced around the old fictitious twelve-person house.

Existing owned plots,levels,balances,loans and construction are retained. A committed catalog4 day keeps its original frozen rules/rates and settles once;new days use household rules. Missing legacy rule fields are normalized from the original receipt revision. A client cannot downgrade a new day to legacy rules. The original catalog4 fixture remains in Data solely for migration tests. No compensating grant or silent city reset is used.

This is a deliberately simplified game household model,not an individual citizen/labor/household-income simulation. Full financial balance,visitor demographics,workforce disruptions and long-term account/economy limits remain part of the broader unfinished product. The demographic correction does not establish retention,revenue or complete societal realism.

## Superseding empty-start and individual-profile revision

The operator subsequently required no default house and meaningful demographic differences. Catalog6/city-rules3 supersedes the fixed-four-person population assumption above. New accounts start with200Cash,construction stock,empty land and zero residents. The player pays for the first home and café;early founding purchase checks retain the necessary cash and resources rather than permitting a dead end. These are real construction/operating transactions,no tutorial-only balance injection.

A dwelling holds up to4 people;actual households vary:families,young couples,retired couples,single adults and shared homes. Stable identities/preferences derive from property and dwelling identity,moving or renaming cannot reroll them. Age advances with a365-operating-day year. Working-age residents are18–64;children and retirees do not staff jobs. Coffee requires an adult with that preference;shopping,services and entertainment consume a bounded household daily budget after rent. Gender is visible identity and never a wage or price multiplier. Capacity is not occupancy;empty dwellings earn no rent.

Existing cities are preserved. Occupied legacy homes retain their four-person families instead of losing residents on migration. Committed catalog4 and5 days keep their original frozen financial/demographic rules;future days use profiles. The legacy fixtures are migration-only Data assets,not runtime catalog alternatives. Validation rejects downgrading a new committed day to legacy rules.

This remains a simplified daily budget/household model. Individual salaries,household bank accounts,births/deaths/migration preferences and complete visitor demographics are not implemented. Do not present it as a full agent-based society or validated commercial economy.
