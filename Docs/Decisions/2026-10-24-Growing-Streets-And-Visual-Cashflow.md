# Growing streets and readable business decisions

The operator’s latest24October request supersedes the earlier full continuous street grid. Start with a regional highway. Add local streets for actual property/construction access and the routes between those properties. Highway access is public infrastructure rather than a free player-owned business. It creates no residents,visitor capacity,inventory or income. Housing occupancy,construction costs,port capacity and all settled finances retain their existing domain rules.

CityStreets derives unique orthogonal edges from current and pending property footprints. One stem joins the first property to the highway;property frontages and directed horizontal-then-vertical routes join the rest. This preserves the domain’s Manhattan distance for every displayed local customer route,including remote businesses. No manual road fees,rerouted travel economy or save migration is introduced. More developed areas can gain more road segments as genuine routes require them. Player-editable roads and traffic-dependent economic travel remain future work.

The terrain draws only this local network plus the single highway extending beyond land. Highway ambient cars use two opposing lanes and the existing full-body collision separation. They do not pretend to be economic arrivals. Customer,coffee and payment tokens remain generated only from real operating events on local streets. Empty plots are visually quieter. A native highway sign opens a short explanation of housing and trade access.

Business inspection replaces a dense accounting paragraph with a two-direction service/payment diagram and income,costs and net figures. The cost figure opens the full actual operating/import/tax breakdown. Home inspection shows actual households and residents. Real construction time drives the progress bar. Price controls show the actual per-sale cash price rather than abstract Economy/Standard/Premium labels. Investment comparisons separate current and post-build next-day net and retain every material requirement and labor/permit cost. The ledger separates monetary rows from visual coffee-demand/power-capacity meters.

No economic outcome,claim,purchase price,reward or billing authority is invented by these changes. Android interaction/readability/audio/video and bounded-camera recovery are the runtime acceptance path. Existing reports stay historical until a current signed build is replayed. Human engagement and commercial outcomes require human/product evidence.
