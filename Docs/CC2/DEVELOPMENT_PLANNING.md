# Player-selected development plans

The player chooses a permanent business and its next authored level. This is a saved preference,not a reward or proof of completion. Construction completion is the condition that satisfies it. Choosing or changing a plan neither spends nor grants anything. A completed goal remains visible until the player chooses another.

The business view compares current and next production using the same fixed-point rate as actual accrual. It lists owned,required and missing amounts,real storage,time and unlock conditions. It explains rental income for owned homes and distinguishes an unbuilt business from ownership. An affordable project promotes construction; a shortage promotes acquisition routes. A busy builder and locked franchise show their actual constraints.

Material guidance offers real unfinished free-city rewards,an eligible saved-layout delivery,an existing delivery return,and owned production where applicable. It quotes actual first-clear rewards and dispatch fees and exposes slot/day limitations. It does not imply replay farming. Production respects unfinished construction settlement and the eight-hour/storage caps. Collecting output or a delivery through the guidance returns to the same project. Back from material guidance preserves the chosen target. The city desk uses the empire Cash wallet and renewable deliveries in this mode.

`DevelopmentPlanning` is shared by Unity and the service's read-only development quote. Presentation cannot grant resources. The backend derives requirements,rates and primary actions through the exact C# domain instead of a second Python cost calculation. Source annotations remain advisory and every write is revalidated. Premium/purchase routes are absent until their real fulfillment is implemented. The local preference is not yet a server-synchronized goal.
