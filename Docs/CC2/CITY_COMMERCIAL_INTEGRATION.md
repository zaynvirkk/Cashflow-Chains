# Commercial integration with the persistent city

This maps Bible I9/I10 and T08.5 to the operator's one-city direction. It describes actual dependency points and remaining work,not active paid offers. New real-money commerce remains inactive. In-game dollar amounts are fictional Cash,not fabricated store prices.

| Demonstrated player need | Working earned/ordinary route | Intended optional benefit | Remaining requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A useful second project while the builder works | Keep operating/planning;develop an owned construction company for an earned second builder | Permanent additional builder | Server entitlement/catalog,real platform metadata,verified fulfillment and restoration |
| A selected construction project lacks timber/steel | Farm/company production or an actual Cash market order;return to the project | Appropriate materials/choice reward | Unified paid bundle fulfillment and source eligibility |
| An expansion has a genuine cash shortfall | Improve margins,wait for operation,or compare optional in-game financing with disclosed repayment | Relevant progression resources | Verified premium inventory and product routes;no sale keyed to unpaid bills |
| Stock repeatedly reaches its storage bound | Develop owned warehouses and use real surplus through ports | Additional capacity/convenience | Actual entitlement modifier and useful-capacity eligibility |
| Imports/exports reach useful throughput limits | Develop port/logistics infrastructure,stock and utilities | Additional freight/capacity or acceleration | Fleet model and production purchase path |
| A chosen business can support more output | Compare staffing,prices,stock,power and an upgrade | Development/manager/equipment resources | Full manager/equipment/reward catalog and authoritative fulfillment |
| A player values the city they named/built | Retain placements,expand and develop owned buildings | Original cosmetic themes/building looks | Current-city skin application,catalog eligibility and real billing |
| A seasonal/recurring objective is genuinely active | Ordinary city actions and a useful free reward track | Premium pass or relevant event resources | Real event/reward authority,clock,content and fulfillment;do not display a fake pass |

When the player can already afford a valid investment,the action is construction/upgrade. Source panels first explain production,operating income,existing material stock and published earned-Cash routes. A loan is a separate fictional financing decision with risk;it is not a real-money purchase and its proceeds do not count as commercial revenue.

Early presentation preserves the intent of the original first-three-puzzle rule: no unsolicited real-money interruption during founding,first business construction and first supported profitable operations. A future offer service must additionally persist one/session and one/24h early introduction limits,expiry/stock/ownership,declines and return context. Static voluntary detail access is separate. No popups cover manipulation,payout or earned reward collection. Current absence of paid prompts does not prove that this offer service has been implemented.

Do not add inert paid buttons to every screen. Only configured products with real price metadata,eligible utility and verified delivery can enter the paid route list. No paid chance results from editable local state. Existing legacy product IDs/entitlements remain preserved;they do not constitute a finished commercial catalog for the new city. App-account reconciliation,Google Play verification,restores/refunds,sandbox payment journeys,consent/privacy and actual player conversion/retention remain open.
