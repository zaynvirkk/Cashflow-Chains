# Preliminary180-day economy report

**Synthetic construction/production projection only. Not a shipping balance signoff or evidence of player spending.**

200,000 synthetic accounts across20 cohorts, paired with200,000 no-purchase counterfactuals. Four activity schedules (8/15/45/90minutes/day),five lifetime spending ceilings. Each scenario runs180days;5% have a14-day hiatus. Willingness-to-pay and acceptance are assumptions. No purchase is forced merely because a budget is available.

The first five-tier catalog fails the long-horizon content and sink checks. All accounts reach the50 authored business upgrades; Cash accumulates to the provisional100,000 collection ceiling. This blocks activation of new revenue catalogs. Adding higher prices alone would not repair the missing useful progression.
