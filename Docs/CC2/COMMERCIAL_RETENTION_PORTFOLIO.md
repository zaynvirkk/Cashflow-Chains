The rewards catalog lives in both identical balance-v1 files, revision7. All claims use `EmpireEngine` and the existing atomic save/ledger. Local free rewards are offline progress, not a secure paid-currency authority. Public service uses authenticated account and server clock; clients cannot inject reward amounts, dates, ownership or random rolls.

Release gates: real Google Play products and localized prices; durable server transaction binding; full account synchronization/recovery; refunds and revocations; payment sandbox acceptance; region/age handling for paid randomness; complete manager/franchise utility before selling their resources. Monetization/retention uplift is unmeasured until actual player cohorts are available. Do not interpret a synthetic economy simulation as revenue evidence.
