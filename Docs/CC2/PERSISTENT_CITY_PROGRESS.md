# Persistent-city rebuild — work in progress

23October2026. The operator's later one-city direction supersedes isolated puzzles as the primary presentation. See Decisions/2026-10-23-One-Persistent-City.md and Decisions/2026-10-23-Distance-And-Travel.md. Both original handoffs and the complete Bible remain preserved. This is not a launch or revenue acceptance report.

Implemented domain slice: one named,expandable city; retained owned placements; quick initial construction/trading followed by longer operating days; actual materials and builder capacity; bounded population tied to housing/jobs/utilities; finite daily coffee/shopping/services/entertainment demand; staffing/capacity/wages; prices and NPC competition; beans,cups,produce,timber,steel and retail goods; published imports; bounded surplus exports; power generation/grid costs; sales tax and permits; deterministic seasons; port/hotel visitor capacity; optional fixed-fee financing,scheduled repayment,arrears and early repayment; optional marketing,supplier agreements and a conditional sponsorship. A developed construction company supplies materials and an earned additional builder. Legacy property,pending construction,earned balances and proofs are retained; old accrued production is settled once and cannot be claimed again through its former path.

Every financial mutation uses the existing validated,idempotent ledger with an atomic city projection. Trading plans are snapshotted and fingerprint-bound. Mid-day changes apply the following day. Next-day forecasting projects current-day closing stock,bills and debt without changing the real account. Current affordability uses current unreserved stock,not predicted income. Purchases of materials have actual prices and return to the suspended project. Long absence is capped at8hours of simulation; the same timestamp cannot regrant results. There is no skip/fast-forward action.

Rendering work: original building art on owned plots,roads/sidewalks/crossings,separated traffic with collision checks,population/operating-day HUD,business margin detail,real city miniature on the city desk,property editing,pan/zoom,attached expansion areas and incoming cash/outgoing coffee/service flows. Both the regular launcher and development player enter the same city model; development saves/label remain isolated. New paid commerce is inactive until authoritative real fulfillment is connected. Old fixed puzzles remain in the domain and archive for compatibility; they are not the primary sequence of new cities.

Verification so far:148 Unity cases pass,including legacy outcomes,travel conservation,traffic separation,city finances,loans/materials/personnel/agreements,offline bounds,next-day projections and migration.20 local API cases passed on development and independently published .NET10 runtimes before the final migration/projection changes; refresh them before a current-source API claim. Native partial runs actually built the first café,waited the3-second build and5-second operating day,earned its supported revenue,added housing and retained the city. Failed render,input-driver,navigation and short-text cases are preserved under Artifacts/CityWorldReplay. The final full native replay is pending after repairs. No edited save or clock is accepted as native evidence.

Still open: final native/device/performance acceptance; airports and additional industries; full manager/research/collection/reward/live-event systems; player-selected specialization and richer district/content progression; complete economic balance/long-horizon validation; production account sync and purchase verification; real store catalog/pricing,sandbox payments and privacy review; human comprehension/enjoyment/retention and commercial outcomes. Generic employees and local agreements are not a claim that the complete manager/LiveOps systems exist. An implemented economic modifier is not a complete societal simulation. Do not call this the completed final game.
