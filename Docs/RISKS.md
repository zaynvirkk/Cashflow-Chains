# Risks and decisions

- P0 release blocker: this is a mechanic slice; full content, commercial services and real device acceptance are unimplemented/unverified.
- P1 product risk: engagement, retention, payment conversion and acquisition economics unknown. No revenue claim follows from these builds.
- P1 UX risk: physical phone touch/notch/thermal, novice comprehension and visual approval require actual testing.
- No accounts, ad SDK, analytics upload, purchases, publishing or paid assets in this milestone. Local telemetry/captures carry no commercial outcome claims.
- Original code-drawn flat shapes are deliberately a small mechanic prototype; production art gate remains pending.
- Local save checksums detect accidental corruption, not a client-side anti-cheat boundary. No competitive leaderboard.
- Use the installed Unity 6000.3.26f1 LTS with template-supplied URP and Input System. Local git only; no remote creation.

- Proposed free distribution after an approved release: organic store discovery and truthful short clips captured from gameplay. Neither acquisition nor conversion has been tested. No paid acquisition is authorized.
