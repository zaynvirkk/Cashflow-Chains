## Critical implementation order
1. Read `03_SHARED_TECH_MONETIZATION_LAUNCH.md`, `04_CODEX_AGENTS_AND_WORKFLOW.md`, and **one** game master specification.
2. Make **two independent Unity projects/repositories**. Share a versioned commercial library only after one project proves it necessary. Do not create a monorepo of interdependent unfinished games.
3. Build a gray-box mechanic with automated tests, then a production-style 2D asset pipeline and the first-session slice.
4. Verify that the game stays interesting *without* purchases before adding the monetization product catalog.
5. Implement the full ship checklist, stage external playtests, measure retention and actual sales. Only then expand content or pay for user acquisition.

## Mandatory design constraints
- **Strict pure 2D**: fixed orthographic camera; top-down flat boards and flat illustrated backgrounds; no 3D objects, perspective buildings, isometric tiles, faux-3D mesh assets, or Asset Store art. 2D layering/glow/particles permitted, but must not suggest a 3D camera.
- All artwork is original, generated by code/vector tooling, licensed generation pipeline or commissioned through our own tool usage; no copyrighted character, distinctive existing-game art, or third-party asset reuse. Fonts and SDK licenses must be documented.
- Portrait mobile layout, one-thumb usability where practical, landscape not required for v1.
- Local-first single player. Internet is required only for optional services and verified real-money purchases. No dependence on multiplayer network effects.
- Never place purchases in front of the initial tutorial. Free play is complete and enjoyable. No deliberately unwinnable levels, fake urgency, fake discounts, or undisclosed randomness.
- These files describe the desired **shipping product**. A document alone cannot guarantee polish, app-store approval, profitability or human testing. Every milestone has an objective verification gate.

## Terminology
**Launch candidate** = feature complete and passing internal tests. **Store-ready** = metadata, account configuration, purchase tests, privacy and accessibility reviewed. **Live** = approved and published on a store. **Profitable** = measured net lifetime contribution greater than attributable acquisition/operating costs; not a design claim.
