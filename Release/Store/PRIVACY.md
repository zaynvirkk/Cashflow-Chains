# Cashflow Chains Privacy Policy

Effective: 21 October 2026  
Developer: Zayn Virk  
Contact: zaynvirkk@gmail.com

Cashflow Chains is a free offline puzzle game. It does not require an account. The game contains no advertisements, in-app purchases, analytics service or social features.

The game stores progress, settings, earned fictional coins and saved city layouts locally on your device. These files are used to resume your game. The game does not send them to the developer or operate a cloud-save service. Android may include local app data in its device backup according to your device settings.

The game does not request access to contacts, location, camera or microphone. It does not sell personal data. Diagnostic screenshots, audio captures and test logs produced by developer testing require explicit launch arguments; ordinary players do not upload diagnostic data through the game.

You can reset progress in Settings after confirmation or remove local app data through Android settings. Uninstalling may remove progress. Device backups are controlled separately by your device/account settings.

If you contact support by email, the developer receives the information you choose to send, including your email address, and uses it to respond to your request. Do not send sensitive personal information or passwords.

This policy may be updated if the game changes. Material changes will be reflected in an updated effective date. For questions, contact zaynvirkk@gmail.com.
