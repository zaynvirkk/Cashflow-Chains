# Cashflow Chains privacy policy draft

Prepared 21 October 2026. Developer: Zayn Virk. Support and privacy contact: zaynvirkk@gmail.com.

Cashflow Chains is a single-player puzzle game. The core campaign works offline without registering an account. It has no advertising SDK, forced advertisements or social features.

The game stores progress, settings, fictional city coins, cosmetic choices, purchased entitlements and a transaction-reference ledger on your device. These files let you resume play and avoid duplicate purchase delivery. Raw payment-card details are not requested or stored by the game. Android may include local app data in device backup according to your device settings.

Optional digital purchases in the Android edition use Google Play through Unity In-App Purchasing 5.4.4. Product prices come from Google Play. Purchase processing involves the platform and SDK and their data handling practices; the earlier free-only policy claiming that the game contains no purchases is superseded. Permanent purchases can be restored through the same store account after verification. Consumed gems and draft tickets are local to this save and do not have cross-device restoration or a cloud wallet.

The game does not request contacts, precise location, camera or microphone. Developer diagnostic screenshots, sound-output captures and test logs require explicit review launch arguments; normal players do not upload those captures through gameplay. No Unity Analytics SDK or advertising identifier integration is enabled by this project.

Reset in Settings archives the local save after confirmation. You can remove local data through Android settings or uninstall the app. Device backups and platform purchase records are controlled separately by their providers. Refunds and platform purchase management use Google Play's processes; resetting a game does not cancel a platform transaction.

If you contact support, the developer receives the information you choose to email and uses it to respond. Do not include passwords or payment-card details. Contact zaynvirkk@gmail.com about support or privacy requests.

## Release review still required

This is not a published policy URL. Before enabling the store, review the exact native billing path and all SDK data categories with the actual Play Console configuration. Unity's IAP 5.4+ documentation lists installation/session/device/country and player identifiers and additional D2C/analytics fields; do not declare the build to be a no-data app merely because Unity Analytics/D2C authentication are not called. D2C, Unity Authentication sign-in, third-party payment providers and advertising are not configured here. Complete accurate Data safety declarations for the shipped SDK behavior and obtain the operator's privacy approval.
