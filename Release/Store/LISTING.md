## Full description

A cafe needs customers. A farm has supplies. One smart connection brings your little city to life.

Cashflow Chains is a city-building puzzle game about making connections that matter. Move buildings, turn delivery arrows and follow people, goods and energy as they travel through your network. Preview the result for free, improve your plan, then activate your chain and watch the city pay out.

Start with three bite-sized lessons, then solve 72 cities across six districts. Discover 24 building types, save your favourite ideas as blueprints, tackle a daily city and earn new palettes through play.

Every city is free. Play offline with no account, advertisements or in-app purchases. Settings include separate music and sound controls, reduced motion, high contrast and haptics.

All cash and coins are fictional game values. This game does not offer real-money earnings or cash redemption. Progress is stored on your device.
