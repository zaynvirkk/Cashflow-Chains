## Full description

A cafe needs customers. A farm has supplies. One smart connection brings your little city to life.

Cashflow Chains is a city-building puzzle game about making connections that matter. Move buildings, turn delivery arrows and follow people, goods and energy as they travel through your network. Preview the result for free, improve your plan, then activate your chain and watch the city pay out.

Start with three bite-sized lessons, then solve 72 core cities across six districts, plus optional Harbor and Finance expansions. Discover 32 building types across the full collection, save your favourite ideas as blueprints, tackle a daily city and earn new palettes through play.

The 72-city core campaign is free. Play offline without an account or forced advertisements. Optional Google Play purchases include cosmetics, extra blueprint slots, local consumable gems/draft rerolls and 32 additional tested puzzle layouts across two expansion districts. Settings include separate music and sound controls, reduced motion, high contrast and haptics.

All cash and coins are fictional game values. This game does not offer real-money earnings or cash redemption. Progress is stored on your device.
