# A toy city that comes alive

The complete23October [Game Bible](Docs/GameBible/CASHFLOW_CHAINS_FINAL_GAME_BIBLE.md), Part I and T12, now governs this summary. Player identity and choice, visible before/after consequences, persistent city evolution and genuine return goals belong to the core experience. Decorative motion or a successful UI script alone does not establish those outcomes.

Current Cashflow direction follows the operator's colorful city reference: lush planted plots, chunky miniature architecture, teal night roads, pink cafes, emerald homes, warm windows, mint deliveries and a gold activation control. The earlier blue-hour iteration is preserved as history. Black Bag is on hold.

Buildings dominate the playfield. Native roads separate square plots and show real port directions. Full-screen portrait and desktop layouts use the whole safe area: central board with surrounding context and controls on wide screens, stacked HUD/board/tray/actions on phones. There is no fixed portrait image floating inside a desktop frame.

A custom illustrated CASHFLOW CHAINS wordmark establishes identity; Bungee gives actions and scores a game-like voice. Uncondensed Barlow Regular/SemiBold keeps explanations readable. Numbers, prices, building labels and instructions are native TextMeshPro text, never baked into a concept image. Alpha-bound metadata crops atlas assets without changing their source pixels.

Presses compress and rebound immediately, with a dry press cue followed by a release cue and particles. Dragging lifts a floating sprite above its faded source, smooths the follow motion and magnetizes only to a legal plot. Landing answers with a snap, spring and burst. Reduced motion removes decorative drift and shortens motion while retaining functional placement feedback.

Every displayed route comes from a real forecast shipment. People, goods and energy have distinct colors. During resolution, native resource tokens travel those paths and actual sales emit money particles and floating amounts. Music ducks under important cues. The persistent wallet changes only through authoritative domain rewards; decorative code cannot award currency.

The first city teaches one useful connection. The second teaches a delivery arrow. The third asks for both. Free forecasts and Undo make experimentation safe; the inspector describes the selected building's actual role or missing supply. Three-star goals and alternate legal layouts offer room to improve after clearing a city. Payouts distinguish puzzle cash from city coins earned through new stars.

Production art, fonts, original synthesized audio and exact prompts are documented in `Docs/ASSET_PROVENANCE.md`. Accepted native/Android captures, frame/audio checks and source-bound reports belong in `Docs/MOBILE_ACCEPTANCE.md` and `Docs/REVIEW.html`. Scripted success is engineering evidence; real-player enjoyment and retention require human playtests.
